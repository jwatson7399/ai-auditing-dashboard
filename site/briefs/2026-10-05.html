The job

Output speed is how fast a model writes its answer once it starts: tokens (word pieces) per second after the first chunk arrives. AA reports the median over the past 72 hours on the provider’s own API. Time to first token is the wait before anything appears, which for a reasoning model includes its hidden thinking.

What changed

Nothing about Sol’s quality. This tells you that at Max thinking, Sol is a cheap-per-point but slow pick: fine for batch work you can leave running, less pleasant for live back-and-forth. Speed depends on the provider and the day’s load, so treat it as a snapshot.

    Caveats

    Super Intelligence Force: WSJ exclusive plus AP confirmation, no official charter yet. The 120-day mandate and Clayton’s quotes come from the WSJ interview (Oct 3); Trump’s confirmation and the FTC / Pentagon / OPM members come from AP (via PBS, Oct 4). “AI czar” is the Journal’s framing (Reuters attributes it to a senior White House official); AP doesn’t use it. Vice chairs, the wider member and adviser list, and the charter quote are WSJ-only details we read through Reuters and AFP relays. We found no charter, order or membership list on whitehouse.gov. Separate from the Sep 29 voluntary accord.

    Norway AI glasses: planned bill, not law. The government says it will bring a bill to the Storting “as soon as possible” (our translation from Norwegian); no date. The listed places are possibilities, which devices count is still being studied, and private use continues.

    IWF figures are still images analysts assessed, not total prevalence online (the Guardian says still images only). The policy ask differs by outlet: EU Child Sexual Abuse Regulation in the IWF release; UK “binding legislation on AI” in the Guardian’s quote.

    VISTA is author-reported and not peer-reviewed. It’s a harness around existing models, run on the 25 public ARC-AGI-3 games only; the authors can’t rule out that the games were in the models’ training data, and ARC Prize hasn’t verified it. Don’t compare it with the ARC-AGI-3 card, which shows ARC Prize–run Semi-Private scores under the Standard harness.

    GPT-6 Astra / StarSkirmish is held: one organiser’s X posts, no run log, no OpenAI comment. “Cheated” and “frustrated” are the organiser’s words.

    Speed is not quality. AA Output Speed is output tokens per second after the first chunk, the median over the past 72 hours on OpenAI’s first-party API. It varies by provider and load; don’t blend it with the Intelligence Index or accuracy cards.

    Carried scores. Apart from the speed card, the job cards and the CE board show the scores from the Oct 4 brief (see each card’s as-of date). Rows stay in score order, so ranks can skip or sit out of sequence. Agent Arena Net Improvement ≠ Elo; Text/Vision are preference Elo, not accuracy. Prefer AA / Vals / LMArena / Epoch for decisions; do not blend harnesses. Scores within ~3 points are within noise on most % boards; Elo / NI CIs matter on LMArena.
