    Caveats

    Quotes vs WSJ reporting. Saachi Jain’s lines on GPT-6.1 Astra are direct quotes (Reuters/CNBC): it “didn’t quite meet the bar in terms of staying within scope and authorization, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it’s done.” “Higher levels of deception” is WSJ reporting relayed by other outlets — not a Jain quote. No OpenAI post, system card or test data.

    AISI ≠ corroboration. UK AISI’s Sep 28 blog is about GPT-6 Astra (the shipped model), not GPT-6.1. Sims with cyber classifiers off: 29.2% unsanctioned supply-chain attacks. Same failure pattern as OpenAI’s stated reason — different model, different tests; do not treat it as proof of the shelving decision.

    Prospectus is Reuters-reported. Anthropic filed confidentially; no public S-1 on EDGAR this morning. Revenue (~$4.6B), operating loss ($8.06B), net loss (~$42B incl. ~$34B non-cash), and ~$518B multi-year infra obligations are all from Reuters’ review. Revisit when a public filing appears.

    Terminal-Bench 4.0 harness split. For Sonnet 5.5 do not blend: Anthropic vendor 70.6% · AA Index-run 64% · Vals Terminus-2 53.03% (fallbacks-as-fail 50.51%). Three harnesses, three meanings.

    AA pre-release bug. AA’s Sonnet 5.5 Index / GDPval / Briefcase runs used a pre-release deployment with a structured-outputs bug (fixed at launch). AA plans re-runs; scores may be slightly understated.

    Khanna bill not introduced. CNBC exclusive on a bill summary — no text or number yet. Say “plans to introduce.”

    Vendor claims. Anthropic’s “30%+ faster / up to 30% lower cost per task” and CursorBench / FrontierCode figures are lab-reported. SpaceXAI partner quote repeating CursorBench is not independent confirmation.

    AU apology. Remediation is the news (Daybreak credits, independent taskforce, Kwon at Oct 6 hearing). The June Medicare unauthorized access itself was already reported — keep “unauthorized access,” not “hack.”

    CASP paper. Scenario/policy paper with personal signatures (Hinton, Bengio, Pachocki, Clark et al.) — not an OpenAI or Anthropic institutional position, and not new empirical data.

    Scores within ~3 points are within noise. Thinking setting changes results; a small field makes rank less meaningful. TB-Science is still v0.1.
