The job

Watch real agent sessions for three concrete failures: acting beyond permissions, putting words in the user’s mouth, and claiming unfinished work is done — then score how rarely those show up.

What changed

There’s now a public, preliminary preference-board-adjacent safety skim for agents. Use it to shortlist for unsupervised tool use, not as a full alignment audit. It doesn’t replace Cyber Index, LAB-AA, or your own evals.

Where they land

    Don’t mix harnesses: Agent Arena Net Improvement ≠ Elo and ≠ pass-rate agent benches. LMArena Text/Vision Elo ≠ accuracy %. LMArena WebDev Elo ≠ Vibe Code Bench. AA Terminal-Bench 4.0 ≠ Vals TB 4.0 mini-swe-agent ≠ Anthropic vendor TB 4.0. Streaming WER is lower-better and not an LLM score. Epoch ECI ≠ AA Intelligence Index: different scales, don’t blend. Alignment Index ≠ Agent Net Improvement. LAB-AA v1.1 ≠ v1.0. Trusted-access Cyber ≠ public Cyber. Scale HSS ≠ LMArena Vision Elo. CyberGym-E2E-AA is one of three parts of the AA Cyber Index, so don’t read them as two separate wins. LMArena image Elo ≠ Google’s internal model-card Elo.

    Caveats

    Open letter vs OpenAI’s response. The researchers deny being the Information leak source and working outside their job mandates; OpenAI has not formally answered the letter. A spokesperson cites a “pattern of misconduct”; an internal memo says the firings were “not about raising safety concerns.” Names and asks are from the letter PDF; OpenAI’s wording is via TechCrunch.

    OSS Scanner figures are company-reported. The 85/97 (88%) check was on “an early version,” found one false positive, and reports go out without human triage. No 90-day window on unvalidated findings.

    Influence ops. Dark Clark / Bogus Bylines attribution, Breakout Scale ratings and reach are OpenAI’s own assessment.

    Usage Policy. Effective Nov 12; the removal of the blanket ban on personalized vote/campaign targeting is a loosening, alongside tighter influence/weapons/surveillance rules.

    AMIE Lancet study. Single-site feasibility (98 completers), Alphabet-funded; most authors are Google/DeepMind. Full-text limitations weren’t re-read here (bot wall); prefer the paper for methods.

    Alignment Index is preliminary. Three observable signals only; LLM-judged; board stamp Sep 30. Not a full alignment audit.

    LAB-AA v1.1 ≠ v1.0. Hallucination gate and three-judge panel change the headline; don’t compare with older All-Pass rates. Hallucination checker is GPT-6 Sol (high).

    Cyber trusted-access. Daybreak Blue is GPT-6 Sol with fewer refusals for verified defenders — not a new public model. Never rank trusted-access rows with public ones.

    WebDev / Agent. Haiku WebDev has only 978 votes. Mistral Large 4’s Agent NI is negative, with the worst tool-hallucination signal on the board (−18.6 ±3.3). The Agent top five are from the Oct 8 board and remain tied.
