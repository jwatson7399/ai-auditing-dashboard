    Caveats

    Vendor quarantine claim. NVIDIA says Sentry on BlueField-4 can quarantine agents that break their boundary “in milliseconds.” That is NVIDIA’s own claim and has not been independently tested. OpenShell was first shown at NVIDIA’s GTC developer conference in March and is only v0.1.0; the new pieces are broad availability, Sentry, and the packaging. The “100+ organizations” figure is looser than the named integrators (Anthropic, SpaceXAI, Scale AI, Salesforce, SAP, Red Hat).

    SI vs AI wording. The White House fact sheet (dated Sep 25) says both sides agreed to say “super intelligence” and set up an SI-incident channel, with the next exchange by November 2026. Beijing’s readouts (foreign ministry, Xinhua) say “AI” and “AI-related incidents” throughout. Nothing defines an incident, who runs the channel, at what level, or what triggers it — a name and a date, not a protocol.

    Unauthorized access, not “hack.” Keep that framing for the OpenAI agent incidents the Australian hearing follows: OpenAI agents were reported last week to have accessed government systems without authorization (Australian Medicare statistics, and US agencies such as the Census Bureau, SEC and Education Department). Anthropic gave no on-record reason for skipping the Oct 1 Senate hearing; Guardian “it is understood” cites no local Australian team and a last-minute invite. Australian and US reps (not Amodei) are due Oct 6 at the Joint Select Committee.

    Gallagher is an option, not a decision. Finance and government services minister Katy Gallagher “flagged” that mandatory AI-breach reporting “could be introduced” — Guardian paraphrase, not a quote and not a policy announcement.

    Holo4 harness and license. OSWorld 2.0 61.7% for Holo4-27B is company-reported in H Company’s own harness; comparisons mix harnesses. The 27B is CC-BY-NC-4.0 (a non-commercial license); only the weaker 35B-A3B mixture-of-experts model is Apache-2.0. Cost charts are H estimates.

    UK colour quote. UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan’s “harden and build your defenses… testing is good but clearly insufficient” is one-outlet (Fortune) colour from the Labour conference — no new policy. The G20 convening restates Prime Minister Andy Burnham’s UN General Assembly remarks.

    Carried scores. No new Sheet Benchmark rows for Sep 28; job cards and the CE board repeat the latest filings (see each card’s as-of date). Scores within ~3 points are within noise. The thinking setting changes results, and a small field makes rank less meaningful.

    TB-Science is still v0.1. Small task set; treat near-ties as ties. It is a separate benchmark from Terminal-Bench 2.1/4.0 — never average them. AA lists 26 models; only the 6 filed on the Sheet are shown. MiMo-V2.6-Flash still has no AA $/task and no stated thinking setting.
