Lead · Northern Ireland AI rules

UK guidance limits EU AI Act duties in Northern Ireland from Nov 10

UK guidance published Nov 4 says limited EU AI Act rules start in Northern Ireland on Nov 10 after the planned Joint Committee decision, covering AI closely integrated with physical products placed on that market — not standalone apps or direct duties on general-purpose model providers.

Why it matters: sellers of product-integrated AI into Northern Ireland face phased duties; intimate-image and child-abuse-imagery bans follow Dec 2.

Source: GOV.UK guidance (PDF)

Details

Confirmed (UK government). Guidance dated Nov 4; Written Statement HCWS455 same day. Application date is Nov 10, following the planned Joint Committee decision — not requirements already active.

Scope: under the Windsor Framework, the Act applies only where AI is closely integrated with a tangible product placed on the Northern Ireland market. Standalone software, web apps and GPAI model providers’ direct obligations are generally out of scope.

Dates: Article 5 market-placement prohibitions, Article 4 AI literacy and Article 50(1) interaction disclosure start Nov 10. Prohibitions on generating non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM, and Article 50(2) output marking, start Dec 2. Annex III high-risk requirements follow Dec 2, 2027; Annex I high-risk Aug 2, 2028.

Caveats: guidance is about three days old at publish; the Nov 10 start is the approaching deadline that warrants the lead.

Co‑lead · Crypto research

DeepMind reports AI-assisted gains on restricted RSA-1024 attacks, not on deployed 2048-bit keys

Google DeepMind says human-guided AI agents reproduced a restricted-signing-oracle RSA-1024 experiment with about 3.5× less CPU time, and describes a roughly 30× lower theoretical marginal cost to factor each extra 1024-bit key after heavy shared precomputation.

Why it matters: legacy 1024-bit RSA research advanced; authors say deployed 2048-bit and larger keys are not shown to be at risk.

Source: DeepMind’s post (X)

Details

Vendor says (Google DeepMind). Pushmeet Kohli post dated Nov 6; two working papers on storage.googleapis.com. Led by Dj Dvijotham and Abhradeep Thakurta with Nadia Heninger and Emmanuel Thomé.

Measured: once an attacker controls roughly half or more of a signed block, padding adds no protection. Agents reproduced a prior RSA-1024 benchmark using 391 versus 1,380 CPU core-years for the first root — 3.5× less CPU time on the same algorithm, not a general RSA key break.

Theoretical: the roughly 30× factoring improvement is an unimplemented leading-order estimate versus MNFS for each additional RSA-1024 target after shared precomputation costing about one GNFS factorization — not measured batch throughput.

Caveats: authors say these results do not threaten deployed RSA-2048+ systems; that is a scoped assessment, not a universal security guarantee. No independent replication yet.

Also · Model licence

Baidu releases ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base weights under a licence that excludes the EU, UK and South Korea

Baidu published downloadable weights for ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base (280B total / 14B active, pretraining-only) under a custom licence that excludes the EU, UK and South Korea and requires written authorization above US$50 million related annual revenue.

Why it matters: downloadable weights carry territorial and revenue gates; this is not unrestricted open source.

Source: Baidu’s ERNIE release

Details

Confirmed (Baidu licence text). Release dated Nov 5; weights on Hugging Face as baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base (FP8 and BF16). Licence: ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base Model License Agreement v1.0.

Territory: worldwide except the EU, UK and South Korea; restrictions extend to derivatives and outputs.

Commercial use: at or below US$50M trailing-12-month related-product/service revenue needs a one-time prior notice and “Powered by ERNIE” attribution; above US$50M needs prior written authorization. Related revenue aggregates commonly controlled entities and includes whole products using other technologies.

Caveats: end-of-pretraining checkpoint only (no mid/post-training). Benchmarks are Baidu-run. About two days old; shared for the licence gates, not model superiority or a pick change.

Also · Consumer finance

Anthropic opens a US Personal Finance beta in Claude with read-only bank access

Anthropic began a gradual US Personal Finance beta for Pro, Max and Ultra users, connecting bank and card accounts through Plaid with read-only access so Claude can see balances and up to two years of transactions but cannot move money.

Why it matters: users get account context in-chat; training exclusion covers hub conversations only, and balances may lag.

Source: Anthropic’s article

Details

Vendor says (Anthropic). Article dated Nov 5; gradual US beta on Pro, Max and Ultra — not universal availability.

Access: Plaid supplies read-only account access; Claude cannot move, manage or invest money. For connected accounts it can see bank name, account name/type, last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to 24 months of transactions. Claude does not see bank passwords.

Privacy: conversations inside the Personal Finance hub are excluded from model training; material shared outside the hub follows the user’s privacy settings. Unlinking stops further access but does not erase existing chats, artifacts or shared links.

Caveats: help page warns balances may be a day or two old and duplicate bank logins can double-count accounts. About two days old; useful for privacy and authorization boundaries, not advice quality or regulatory approval.

How we rank
  • Ties: on boards with error ranges, rank = 1 + models whose whole range sits above (LMArena’s rule), so overlaps show “=”. Boards with no ranges are ranked by value only and called “close race” when the gap is small.
  • Tags: Confirmed = official or independent source · Vendor says = the company’s or authors’ own account · Outlet reports = one outlet’s reporting, not yet officially confirmed · Too close to call = tied within ranges.
  • Jobs: each pick keeps its own as-of date and is carried forward until its board updates. Independent boards first (AA = Artificial Analysis, Vals, LMArena, Epoch, Scale, ARC Prize). Harnesses, scales and method versions are never mixed.
