    Caveats

    Accord text source. Full Joint Commitment text is via the Washington Examiner (Trump posted on Truth Social); it is not on whitehouse.gov this morning. AP (via NPR) confirms signatories and that it is voluntary. No penalties, enforcement, auditor standards or public reporting; Trump calls it “morally binding.”

    GPT-6.1 Sol ≠ shelved GPT-6.1 Astra. Sol shipped at DevDay; GPT-6.1 Astra was shelved Sep 28 after OpenAI said it fell short on staying within the scope of what it was authorized to do and on reporting back what it had done. Different models. OpenAI’s launch page does not discuss the cancellation.

    Preparedness is self-assessed. GPT-6.1 Sol’s “Critical” cybersecurity and “High” biological/chemical risk ratings are OpenAI’s own, under OpenAI’s own Preparedness Framework in the system-card addendum — not an external rating.

    Vals Index recomposed Sep 29. Earlier Vals Index rows on file (e.g. old Sol 62.57%, Astra 66.61%) are not comparable to today’s recomposed board (Sonnet 67.04%, Opus 66.97%, GPT-6.1 Sol 61.15%, GPT-6 Sol 57.54%).
