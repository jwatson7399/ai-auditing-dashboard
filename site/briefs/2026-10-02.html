    Caveats

    Murphy–Hawley: announced, not introduced. No bill number or text on Congress.gov; Axios says the senators are “planning to introduce” it. “Prison time” is the senators’ framing; who counts as an “operator,” what “reasonable safeguards” means, and the penalties are unknown until text drops. Distinct from the White House voluntary accord and from Khanna’s still-unintroduced bill.

    Bonta subpoena: investigative step only. Contents aren’t public; no lawsuit or finding; no OpenAI response in the release. California-only and OpenAI-only — separate from the federal FTC industry-wide probe.

    Broadcom / Anthropic loan: Reuters from a confidential prospectus. Not on EDGAR; Broadcom did not comment and Anthropic declined. Every figure is Reuters-reported until a public S-1 (IPO registration filing) lands.

    LMArena WebDev is preference Elo, not accuracy. Don’t blend with Vibe Code Bench or other pass-rate coding scores. Opus leads Astra outright; Sonnet 5.5 Extra-high’s interval (LMArena rank 1–4) overlaps both, so Astra and Sonnet are shown tied 2=. Vote counts on new rows are still low, so ratings can move.

    Project Suncatcher: Google’s word only. Launch and contact confirmed by Google; no Planet or SpaceX statement checked; no in-orbit results yet.

    SynthIDBio is a proof of concept. Paper says resequencing can remove the sequence watermark and constrained relaxation can destroy the structure watermark.

    Strands Decider benches are author-reported on a little-known 231-task set; licence is Apache-2.0.

    SB 574 takes effect Jan 1, 2027 (non-urgency). It is the first statewide statute aimed at lawyers’ and arbitrators’ generative-AI use, not the first state AI law.

    Vendor vs independent benches. Prefer AA / Vals / LMArena / Epoch cards for decisions. Do not blend AutomationBench-AA with Zapier AutomationBench, or AA TB with Vals / Anthropic vendor TB.
