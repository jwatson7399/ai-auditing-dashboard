Cost / who is efficient

Same model, different “think harder” dial. Labs let you turn thinking up or down. Higher settings often burn more money for the same job. This list is one hard test task, cheapest first. Lower $ is better.

Use this today: Prefer a model’s normal thinking unless you have a reason to crank it. For Grok 4.7, normal and max got the same score (46) on this test — max just cost more (~$3.74 vs ~$2.73).

How to read the dial showhide

Normal thinking = the usual setting in apps/APIs (often called “high”).

Max thinking = dial all the way up (often called “xhigh” or “max”). Slower and usually pricier.

Low thinking = dial turned down. Can be much cheaper; score may drop.

If two rows share a model name, they are not duplicates — they are two dial settings. Pick the cheaper one that still does your job.

Before you trust this

Small gaps don’t matter. A couple of points on a 0–100 score is often noise.

Hard mode ≠ what you get in the app. These charts usually use the model’s hardest thinking setting. Everyday chat is often cheaper and a bit weaker.
