How to read the dial showhide

Medium thinking = Anthropic’s default effort on Opus 5.5. Good first setting for coding agents.

High / Extra-high / Max = dial up. Often costs more; score gains shrink after High on several charts.

Low thinking = dial down. Cheaper; score usually drops.

If two rows share a model name, they are not duplicates — they are two dial settings.

Before you trust this

Lab-reported ≠ finished independent audit. Bars come from Anthropic’s announce table (and Zapier / OpenAI-reported peer cells). Artificial Analysis posted a same-day Opus 5.5 article — useful cross-check, different harnesses, different numbers.

Safeguards were on. Anthropic says production cyber/biology fallbacks may have lowered some Opus 5.5 scores (failed safeguards counted as fails on AutomationBench).

Effort matters. Headline Opus 5.5 numbers are mostly adaptive thinking at max (Terminal-Bench uses extra-high). Everyday apps often run Medium — cheaper, sometimes nearly as good.

Small gaps don’t matter. A couple of points is often noise (TB4.0 SE ±2.6 for Opus 5.5; TB-Science SE ±3.5–5).
