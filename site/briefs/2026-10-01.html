    Caveats

    No FTC release yet. Probe confirmed by an FTC spokesperson to Axios and CBS; NYPost broke it; no press release or complaint filed. Start timing differs by outlet. Anthropic and OpenAI have not commented.

    Argon: no model card / FSF finding. Google cites the Frontier Safety Framework only for misuse refusals. No Critical Capability Level (the framework’s danger threshold) finding or linked model card. Access is Fairwind defenders first; paid API/AI Ultra has no date.

    Vendor vs independent benches. Google’s DeepSWE v1.1 77.9% and Zapier AutomationBench 51.3% are Google-run. Prefer AA / Vals cards for decisions. Do not blend AutomationBench-AA (78%, partial-credit, private set) with Zapier AutomationBench.

    Moonshot attribution is OpenAI-only. OpenAI attributes a core distillation cluster to individuals associated with Moonshot AI; no supporting evidence published; Moonshot has not responded; request counts are attempts.

    Transluce LAC attribution hedged. Education probe tied to “oai”-tagged traffic; Transluce does not confidently attribute Library and Archives Canada attempts to OpenAI. No nonpublic access found.

    ARC-AGI-3 harness split. Sol Standard Max 52.73% and Provider Adapter XHigh 96.37% are different harnesses — never blend or rank them against each other. ARC-AGI-1 is near ceiling (98.5%).

    Vals TB 4.0 harness. Current Vals TB 4.0 board uses mini-swe-agent (bash only), not Terminus 2. Do not blend with AA TB 4.0, Anthropic vendor TB, or older Vals Terminus 2 TB 4.0 figures.

    Italy Art. 437-bis. The 10-year maximum applies only to tampering that endangers State security. AI Act high-risk duties it references are delayed to late 2027 / 2028.

    Proofpoint TA419. Attribution is Proofpoint’s; Reuters reports fewer than 10 targets; no confirmation any account was compromised.
