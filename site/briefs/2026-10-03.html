    Caveats

    OpenAI researchers: WSJ exclusive + OpenAI statement. OpenAI on the record says it “parted ways” with three people who “mishandled sensitive information outside established company procedures.” “Fired” is the WSJ’s word. The claim that confidential information went to a third-party AI-safety organisation comes from the WSJ’s sources, not OpenAI. Organisation and researchers’ names are not confirmed here.

    Minnesota / xAI: temporary injunction, not merits. 8th Cir. No. 26-2806 (Oct 2) is a one-line order granting an injunction pending appeal — no opinion, no stated scope, not a First Amendment merits ruling. “First-in-the-nation” is Reuters’ label. Minnesota AG’s office says it will keep defending the law; xAI did not immediately respond (Reuters).

    Apple Full Disk Access: no ship date. Apple says it will require “very explicit user action” because risks “will grow substantially” as AI agents become more autonomous. No macOS version or date; Apple names no app.

    Muse Gadgets: SDKs open, Home Link locked. ESP32/Linux device SDKs are Apache-2.0; Muse access needs a non-commercial SDK token. Muse Home Link runs locked official firmware and can’t be reflashed. Batch of 5,000 is Nat Friedman’s figure, not Meta’s product page. Free to U.S. Muse subscribers, limit one, ships in October.

    Claude Frontier Academy is a company pledge ($100M / 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by end-2027) — not results. First badges expected early 2027.

    Olmo-core 3 is a training-stack speed bench, not model quality (~2.7× Ai2’s own older stack; Apache-2.0; Ai2 + University of Washington).

    Cloudflare Web Search API is open beta through AI Gateway (Ceramic, Exa, Linkup) at provider list prices with no markup — not generally available.

    Agent Arena Net Improvement ≠ Elo. It is a % treatment effect vs the average model from implicit signals in real user agent sessions. Overlapping 95% CIs mean the top five are statistically tied. Text/Vision are crowd-sourced preference Elo, not accuracy.

    Vendor vs independent benches. Prefer AA / Vals / LMArena / Epoch cards for decisions. Do not blend AutomationBench-AA with Zapier AutomationBench, or AA TB with Vals / Anthropic vendor TB.
