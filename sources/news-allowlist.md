# News Scout allowlist

This file is the authority for the News Scout's sources. The Benchmark Scout uses sources/allowlist.md instead, which is narrower. The Blocklist in sources/allowlist.md also applies here.

## Policy and legal

Federal Register, EU official journal and EU AI Act pages, UK government AI publications, FTC, SEC, state attorney general offices, court opinions published by the court itself, NIST.

## Blocklist

Never file, quote, or propose these, in addition to everything on the Blocklist in sources/allowlist.md.

- Aggregators and content farms that restate other outlets without adding reporting.
- LinkedIn posts, Medium posts, and Substack newsletters that are not by a named allowlisted author.
- YouTube videos as a primary source, unless the video is the company's own launch event or an allowlisted tester's own recording.
- Any site whose only content is a summary of an allowlisted source. Link the allowlisted source directly instead.
